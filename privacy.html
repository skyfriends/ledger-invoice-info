Privacy

Ledger requests the Google Gmail read-only permission to find OpenAI billing emails and retrieve original messages and invoice attachments. That permission can read email messages and settings; Ledger’s collector uses only read requests and does not send, delete, archive, or change mail.

Use and storage

Google user data is used only to collect, verify and display invoices and receipt spending. Original evidence and collection records are stored privately on the Mac. Google refresh tokens and client configuration are stored in the Mac login Keychain.

Ledger contacts Google to authorize the selected mailbox, renew access and read billing messages. It downloads original invoice documents from the billing provider when required. The collector does not upload email, invoices or credentials to an app server, analytics service or AI service.

Control

Disable Daily collection in Ledger to stop scheduled scans. Revoke Ledger’s authorization in your Google Account to stop future Google access. Imported local invoices remain on the Mac until the owner removes them; disabling or reconnecting collection preserves them.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
