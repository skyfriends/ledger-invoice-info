The Ledger

A personal macOS app for account usage and invoice tracking.

Ledger collects OpenAI billing emails through a read-only Google connection, verifies original invoice and receipt PDFs, and keeps a deduplicated local spending record.

The daily collector runs on the Mac. It does not need a chat or AI agent, and this website does not process email or invoices.

Only the owner’s explicitly connected billing mailboxes are used. This is a personal application, not a public hosted billing service.
